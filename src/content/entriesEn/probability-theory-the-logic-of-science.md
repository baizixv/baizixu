## About the book

The central claim of *Probability Theory: The Logic of Science* is that probability is not only a mathematical tool for describing random phenomena, but can also be understood as the logic of sound reasoning under incomplete information. Jaynes tries to remove the artificial split between probability theory and statistical inference, bringing Bayesian inference, hypothesis testing, parameter estimation and model comparison into one framework.

Beyond the standard mathematics of probability, the book discusses the principle of maximum entropy, transformation groups, paradoxes and statistical mechanics, and applies probabilistic reasoning across physics, mathematics, economics, chemistry and biology. It suits readers with an advanced undergraduate background in applied mathematics who want a systematic understanding of Bayesian methods and the logic of scientific reasoning.
