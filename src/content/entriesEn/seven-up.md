## Viewing notes

*7 Up* begins at age seven and revisits the same group of British children every seven years, recording their families, education, work, marriages and life choices over decades. It puts individual lives on a timescale of decades and makes visible how class, opportunity and chance shape a life together.

This is a series to watch slowly: not rushing toward a conclusion, but observing how people change over time and how seemingly small choices gradually merge into very different paths.
