## Reading notes

*Influence* is not about how to manipulate people; it helps readers recognise the moment persuasion happens: why “everyone is doing it”, an expert endorsement, or a seemingly tiny commitment can change what we choose.

I am especially interested in how the book describes automatic responses, and how these principles show up in work communication, spending decisions and everyday relationships. Its value is not only in teaching persuasion, but in helping us pause for a moment before deciding.
